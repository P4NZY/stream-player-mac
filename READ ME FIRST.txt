STREAM PLAYER FOR MAC
=====================

The Music Player, for Spotify and SoundCloud: the same retro hi-fi player -
library, playlists, themes, visualizer, lyrics, queue, the compact player that
floats on top - with a switch at the top to flip between the two services.

It runs inside Google Chrome (or Microsoft Edge or Brave), in a window of its
own with its own icon, apart from your everyday browsing. It is a separate app
from Music Player (the Apple Music one): both can be installed side by side.


SETTING IT UP (once)
--------------------
1. Put this whole "Stream Player for Mac" folder anywhere on the Mac
   (Documents, say).

2. Open Terminal (Cmd + Space, type Terminal, press Return).
   Type  bash  and a space, then drag the file  install.sh  from this folder
   into the Terminal window, and press Return. (The Mac needs to be online:
   the installer downloads Spotify's and SoundCloud's own player scripts.)

3. Stream Player opens. With Edge or Brave the player is there straight away.
   With Google Chrome it comes together with Chrome's Extensions page and a
   window with directions, the first time: switch on "Developer mode", click
   "Load unpacked", press Cmd + Shift + G, paste
       ~/Library/Application Support/Stream Player/extension
   press Return, click "Select", close the Extensions page and press Cmd + R.

4. Connect the services - the player walks you through it (ACCOUNT, bottom
   left, for whichever service the switch is set to). See below.

From then on just open "Stream Player" - it is in the Applications folder in
your home folder, in Launchpad and in Spotlight. Drag it to the Dock.


THE SWITCH
----------
At the top, next to the name: SPOTIFY [=] SOUNDCLOUD. Click it (or press
Ctrl + S) to flip to the other service. The library on the left, the browser,
search, the deck and the queue all change over. Whatever was playing on the
service you leave is paused; each keeps its own queue and place, so flipping
back finds it as it was.


SPOTIFY
-------
Spotify lets players like this one in only through an app of your own, made
on Spotify's developer dashboard. It is free and takes about a minute, once:
the player's ACCOUNT page (with the switch on SPOTIFY) shows the steps, with a
button for each:
  1. open the developer dashboard and log in with your Spotify account
  2. Create app - any name; Redirect URI:  http://127.0.0.1:43117/spotify
  3. tick "Web API" and "Web Playback SDK", save
  4. copy the app's Client ID into the player, press CONNECT, and sign in on
     Spotify's own page in the small window that opens.

What to know:
- Playing needs Spotify Premium (Spotify's rule for every player outside its
  own apps), and the app you made only works while your account has Premium.
- Your library is Spotify's: Liked Songs, saved albums, followed artists, your
  playlists. Saving a song, an album or a playlist, adding to a playlist and
  making a new one change your Spotify account, so they show up in Spotify's
  own apps too.
- Spotify only shows other players the songs of playlists that are your own.
  Other people's playlists (and Spotify's) still play - PLAY or SHUFFLE on
  their page - and the queue fills in as they go.
- Spotify no longer gives other players an artist's top songs; an artist's
  page shows their albums and singles.
- Spotify asks for the sign-in again every six months; the player says so.
- While the player is open it is one of your Spotify Connect devices,
  "Stream Player", so your phone can see and control it too.


SOUNDCLOUD
----------
SoundCloud only lets players like this one into its API (search, your own
account's library) when they are registered by SoundCloud Artist Pro
subscribers. What it offers everyone is its own embeddable player - the one
on blogs and band pages - and that is what plays here. So:
- Nothing to sign in to. Every public song, playlist, album and artist plays.
  Go+ songs play as 30-second previews (as they do for anyone signed out).
- "Likes & Saved": put your profile link in (ACCOUNT, with the switch on
  SOUNDCLOUD - it looks like soundcloud.com/yourname) and your public likes
  show up there, along with songs you save in the player.
- Playlists you make, and songs, playlists, albums and artists you save, are
  kept by the player, on this Mac (not on your SoundCloud account).
- Search: paste a SoundCloud link (a song, playlist, album or artist) into the
  search box. To search by words, "Browse SoundCloud" opens SoundCloud in a
  window of its own: open a song, playlist or artist there and the player's
  "Browse SoundCloud" page offers PLAY IT HERE, SHOW IT HERE and SAVE IT HERE.
- "History" lists what you played here.


LYRICS
------
Neither service gives other players lyrics, so they come from LRCLIB
(lrclib.net), a free, open lyrics collection: most are timed line by line and
follow the song. Songs it does not know show "no lyrics". On SoundCloud the
title is often "Artist - Song (Official Audio)"; the player reads it that way.


THE COMPACT PLAYER FLOATS ON TOP
--------------------------------
As in Music Player: COMPACT (or Ctrl + M) puts the small player in a little
window of its own that stays above every other window, whatever app you use.
LIBRARY (or closing it) brings the full player back. The pin key switches the
floating off and on ("Keep on top" under THEMES).


THE VISUALIZER HEARS THE MUSIC
------------------------------
Started from the Stream Player app, the visualizer follows the music of both
services by itself (the browser lets the player's extension listen to its own
window; nothing leaves your Mac). If it ever shows DEMO, it is moving to a
built-in beat because it could not hear the music; it says why on the picture.


STREAM PLAYER IN THE DOCK
-------------------------
Stream Player stays open as long as the player is, so the Dock shows the dot
under it. Click it (or pick it with Cmd + Tab) and the player comes to the
front. Quit it and the player quits too; quit the player (Cmd + Q in its
window) and Stream Player goes too. The browser the player runs in shows in
the Dock as well: it is a second copy of that browser, the player's own.


UPDATING
--------
Copy the newer "Stream Player for Mac" folder over this one and run install.sh
again. If the player is open, install.sh closes it first and opens it again at
the end. Your sign-ins, settings and saved things stay.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- "Stream Player" says something has moved: run install.sh again.
- The window shows an error page instead of the player (Chrome): the
  extension is not switched on - do step 3 again, then Cmd + R.
- Spotify: "playing needs Spotify Premium" - the account (and the developer
  app's owner) needs Premium. "this browser cannot play Spotify" - with Brave,
  check brave://settings/extensions that Widevine is on, then reopen.
- Spotify sign-in says the redirect URI is wrong: in your app's settings on
  the dashboard it must be exactly  http://127.0.0.1:43117/spotify
- SoundCloud likes stay empty: the profile link has to be your public profile,
  and your likes have to be public on SoundCloud.
- To remove it: delete "Stream Player" from your Applications folder and the
  folder ~/Library/Application Support/Stream Player
