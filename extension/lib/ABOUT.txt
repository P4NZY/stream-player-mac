These two files are the services' own, kept here because a browser extension
may only run files of its own (it cannot load scripts from the web):

  spotify-player.js   Spotify's Web Playback SDK loader
                      https://sdk.scdn.co/spotify-player.js
                      It only opens Spotify's player (sdk.scdn.co) in a hidden
                      frame and talks to it; the player itself always comes
                      fresh from Spotify.

  sc-widget-api.js    SoundCloud's Widget API, for driving SoundCloud's
                      embeddable player
                      https://w.soundcloud.com/player/api.js

To bring either up to date, download it again from its address over the copy
here, then run install.sh again.
